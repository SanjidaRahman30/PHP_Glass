PHP Float Glass Industries Limited

Founded under the visionary leadership of Sufi Mohamed Mizanur Rahman, PHP Float Glass Industries Limited represents the gold standard of architectural glass manufacturing in Bangladesh. Located in Chattogram, our plant utilizes advanced float melting technology and high-precision cutting lines to supply both domestic and global projects.

From commercial skyscrapers to modern residences, our glass and aluminium solutions empower architects and builders with unmatched optical clarity, thermal efficiency, and lifelong durability.
